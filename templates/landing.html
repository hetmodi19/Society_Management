Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Living Here

Key questions regarding resident onboarding, gate passes, UPI maintenance, Model Bye-Laws, and amenity bookings.

How do digital visitor passes and Swiggy/Zomato deliveries work? +
Residents can generate a 6-digit OTP guest pass or instant QR code from their portal with 1-click WhatsApp sharing. When your guest, cab (Uber/Ola), or delivery partner arrives at Gate 1 or 2, the security guard terminal validates the pass instantly, sending you a real-time entry notification.
How do I pay monthly maintenance bills and download stamped receipts? +
Bills are automatically generated on the 1st of each month according to your flat's RERA carpet area, parking bays, and 10% statutory sinking fund under Model Bye-Laws. You can pay seamlessly using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) by scanning the dynamic QR code in your resident portal. An official digital receipt with society stamp is generated instantly.
How do I reserve the Banquet Hall, Temple Lawn, or Cricket Turf? +
Log in to the Resident Portal and navigate to the Clubhouse & Amenities module. Select your desired facility (Banquet Hall for Poojas/birthdays, Box Cricket Turf, Badminton Court), choose an available date and time slot, and confirm. Status updates are processed within 24 hours.
How does the 1-Tap SOS Emergency Siren and Otis Lift Rescue operate? +
Clicking the red SOS button broadcasts an immediate audio-visual siren to the Gate 1 security desk with your exact flat number, wing, and elevator location. The guard desk immediately dispatches rapid response personnel and connects with Apollo Ambulance (108), Police (100/112), and Otis Lift Rescue (Ext #1999).
How do I register a tenant, domestic helper, or FastTag vehicle? +
Flat owners can register tenants under CHS Bye-Law guidelines with Non-Occupancy Charges (capped at 10% of service charges) and police verification. You can also add domestic staff (maids, drivers, cooks) to issue biometric daily RFID entry passes and register vehicles for automatic boom barrier clearance.
