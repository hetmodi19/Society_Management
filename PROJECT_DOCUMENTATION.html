2. Executive Summary & Abstract

SmartSociety 360 is an enterprise-grade, full-stack Residential Estate ERP, Gate Security, and Democratic Governance web application built with Python 3.12, Django 5.x, and a custom-engineered Vanilla CSS3 design system. It addresses the critical operational, financial, and security bottlenecks of high-rise residential complexes and Co-operative Housing Societies (CHS).

The platform eliminates fragmented manual registers and un-audited processes by delivering:

  • Automated Financial ERP: RERA carpet-area proportional billing, statutory 10% sinking fund accounting, and 1-tap dynamic UPI QR code settlement with instant PDF GST receipts.
  • Digital Gatekeeper & FastTag: 1-click WhatsApp 6-digit OTP guest passes, delivery partner fast-tracking, digital parcel lockers, and an instantaneous 1-Tap Emergency SOS Siren.
  • Smart Eco-Infrastructure: Conflict-free amenity calendar booking and a 48.5 kW Rooftop Solar EV Hyper-Charging Plaza with live kWh billing.
  • SLA-Tracked Helpdesk: Service ticket management with photo uploads and strict 15-minute emergency technician dispatch.
  • Democratic E-Voting: 1-Flat-1-Vote referendums adhering to the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 Model Bye-Laws.

7. Mathematical & Billing Calculation Models

Base maintenance is computed proportionally against certified RERA carpet area in accordance with statutory guidelines:

Base Maintenance = RERA Carpet Area (sq.ft) × Rate (₹/sq.ft)
Example: 1,850 sq.ft × ₹4.80 = ₹8,880.00
Sinking Fund (10%) = Base Maintenance × 0.10 = ₹888.00
Total Bill = Base Maintenance + Sinking Fund + Parking Fee + EV Levy
